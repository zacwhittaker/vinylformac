Album art on your desktop

Vinyl turns your current Spotify track into a photorealistic turntable wallpaper on your Mac.

Download for Mac

macOS Sonoma or later · 13 MB

🎶

Live turntable

A detailed scene with spinning vinyl, tonearm, and your album art on the sleeve and label — all rendered in real time.

▶️

Follows your music

Artwork updates instantly when you change tracks. The progress bar, elapsed time, and record rotation all stay in sync.

🖥

Lives in the menu bar

Starts silently at login. No dock icon, no window — just your wallpaper and a quiet menu bar icon for controls.

🎨

Click-through

The wallpaper sits behind your icons and windows. It never gets in the way — it just makes your desktop yours.

Spotify connected

Vinyl connects to Spotify with your own API credentials. Create a free app on the Spotify Developer Dashboard, and Vinyl handles the rest.
