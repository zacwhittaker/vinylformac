Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

What Vinyl accesses

Vinyl connects to the Spotify Web API using the user-read-currently-playing scope. This gives it read-only access to the track or episode you are currently playing — specifically the title, artist, album name, album artwork URL, and playback progress.

What Vinyl stores

Your Spotify OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token) are stored locally in your Mac's Keychain. Your wallpaper preference (on/off) is stored in local UserDefaults. No data is stored on any server.

What Vinyl sends

Vinyl communicates only with Spotify's servers (accounts.spotify.com and api.spotify.com) to authenticate and fetch your currently playing track. It does not send data to any other server, does not include analytics or telemetry, and does not collect or transmit any personal information.

No tracking

Vinyl does not track you. There are no analytics, no crash reporters, no ad networks, no third-party SDKs.
