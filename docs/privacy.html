Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

What Vinyl accesses

Vinyl reads playback-change notifications published locally by the Spotify desktop app. These include the current track title, artist, album, Spotify track identifier, and playback progress.

What Vinyl stores

Your wallpaper and setup preferences are stored locally on your Mac. Vinyl does not request or store Spotify passwords, OAuth tokens, or API credentials. No data is stored on any developer-operated server.

What Vinyl sends

Vinyl sends the current Spotify track identifier to Spotify's public embed endpoint to find its album artwork, then downloads that artwork from Spotify's image servers. It does not include analytics or telemetry and does not send playback data anywhere else.

No tracking

Vinyl does not track you. There are no analytics, no crash reporters, no ad networks, no third-party SDKs.
